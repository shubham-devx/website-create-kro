# How to Use Portfolio Bnao

Welcome to **Portfolio Bnao** – the free, no-code portfolio builder! This guide walks you through creating your professional portfolio in 6 simple steps.

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Fill in Your Details
Scroll down to the portfolio builder form and enter your information across 5 sections:

**Your Story**
- Your name (required)
- Your role/title (e.g., "Product Designer")
- Your email
- Mobile number
- Based in (city/location)
- Professional summary (your bio)
- Profile photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP)

**Experience & Education**
- Work experience (one role per line)
- Education (one qualification per line)
- Technical skills (separate with commas)
- Other skills (separate with commas)
- Hobbies & interests (separate with commas)

**Your Work**
- Project details (project name, description)
- Project links (GitHub, live site, etc.)

**Links & Documents**
- LinkedIn and other professional links
- Resume link (PDF or online)
- Certificate links

**Vibe**
- Color theme (Violet, Green, Orange)
- Font style (Modern, Editorial, Classic, Mono)

### Step 2: Watch the Live Preview
As you type, your portfolio updates instantly on the right side of the screen. See exactly how your portfolio will look before downloading.

### Step 3: Add Your Photo
Click "Choose a photo" and select a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image. The image will be:
- Automatically resized to optimize file size
- Embedded in your portfolio (no server uploads)
- Kept on your device

### Step 4: Auto-Save Works
Your work is automatically saved to your browser as you type. You can:
- Close the browser and come back later
- Edit your portfolio anytime
- Your draft will still be there when you return

💡 **Note**: Saved drafts are stored locally in your browser. Clearing your browser's cache will remove them.

### Step 5: Download Your Portfolio
Scroll to the bottom and click **"Make my portfolio"** to download your complete portfolio as a standalone HTML file. The file includes:
- All your information and photo (embedded)
- Professional styling
- No dependencies on external servers
- Fully functional offline

### Step 6: Share or Host
Your downloaded portfolio file can be:
- Emailed to friends, recruiters, or employers
- Uploaded to GitHub Pages (free hosting)
- Hosted on any static hosting service (Netlify, Vercel, etc.)
- Shared directly as an attachment
- Works completely offline – no server needed

## Tips & Best Practices

### Photo Upload
- Use a professional headshot or profile photo
- Keep image file size under 8 MB (though smaller is better)
- JPG format works best for smaller file sizes
- Ensure good lighting and clear visibility

### Writing Your Bio
- Keep it concise (2-3 sentences max)
- Highlight your unique value
- Use keywords relevant to your industry
- Make it personal but professional

### Project Links
- Include working links to live projects or GitHub repos
- Use full URLs (e.g., `https://github.com/username/project`)
- All links open in new tabs
- Invalid links are automatically handled

### Skills
- List skills that matter to your industry
- Order by proficiency or relevance
- Use consistent naming (e.g., "React" not "react")
- Separate multiple skills with commas

## FAQ

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes! All your data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Only you can see your portfolio.

**Q: Can I update my portfolio after downloading?**  
A: Yes, you can always come back and edit your draft, then download an updated version.

**Q: What if I clear my browser cache?**  
A: Your draft will be removed (it's stored locally in your browser). Keep backups of important portfolios!

**Q: Can I host the HTML file on my own website?**  
A: Absolutely! The generated HTML is self-contained. Upload it to any hosting service.

**Q: Is the portfolio mobile-friendly?**  
A: Yes, your portfolio automatically adjusts to look great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

**Q: Can I customize the design beyond color and font?**  
A: The downloaded HTML file is pure HTML/CSS. Advanced users can edit it directly to customize further.

**Q: Is this a free service?**  
A: Yes, 100% free forever. No accounts, no paid features, no surprises.

## Troubleshooting

### "My draft disappeared"
Clear browser cache will remove stored drafts. Always keep backups of important portfolios.

### "The photo didn't upload"
- Ensure it's JPEG, PNG, or WebP format
- File must be under 8 MB
- Try refreshing the page

### "The preview isn't updating"
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
- Check your browser's developer console for errors
- Try a different browser

### "The download button isn't working"
- Check that your browser allows downloads
- Disable any download blockers
- Try a different browser
