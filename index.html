How to Use Portfolio Bnao

1

Fill in Your Details

Scroll down and enter your name, role, contact info, professional experience, education, skills, and project details. You can customize everything.

2

Watch the Live Preview

See your portfolio update instantly on the right as you type. Choose your favorite color theme and font style to personalize your look.

3

Add Your Photo

Upload a profile photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP). It'll be automatically resized and embedded in your portfolio.

4

Auto-Save Works

Your work is automatically saved to your browser. Come back anytime to edit—your draft will still be there.

5

Download Your Portfolio

Click "Make my portfolio" at the bottom to download a complete HTML file. Open it anywhere—no server needed.

6

Share or Host

Email your portfolio file, upload it to GitHub Pages, or use any static hosting service. Your portfolio works completely offline.
